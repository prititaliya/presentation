Overview

WealthWise is an Android-based Wealth Management System developed over multiple iterations, focusing on helping users track and manage their financial activities efficiently. The project evolved through careful planning and iterative development.

Iterative Development

Developed through four iterations with continuous improvement

Team Collaboration

Effective teamwork with regular meetings and clear communication

Project Management

Organized development with detailed documentation and tracking

Vision

Our vision is to create a comprehensive wealth management solution that helps users track their financial activities, analyze spending patterns, and make informed financial decisions while maintaining a clean and intuitive user interface.

"Building a wealth management system that combines simplicity with powerful financial tracking features, making financial management accessible to everyone."

For Everyone

Individual Users

Track personal finances and spending habits

Financial Planners

Monitor and analyze financial activities

Budget Conscious

Manage budgets and track expenses

Features

Account Management

Take control of your financial journey with our comprehensive account management system:

  • Create a secure personal account with email
  • Update your profile information and preferences anytime
  • Edit your personal details and settings as needed
  • Option to safely delete your account and data if needed

Finance Tracking

Keep detailed records of your financial activities with our intuitive tracking tools:

  • Log income and expense entries with comprehensive details
  • View comprehensive activity snapshots and full history
  • Edit or delete any entry with full revision history
  • Real-time balance updates

Finance Analytics

Gain valuable insights into your financial health with advanced analytics:

  • Interactive dashboard with financial health summary and metrics
  • Detailed breakdown of income sources with trend analysis
  • Categorized expense tracking with visual representations
  • Flexible time-frame views: weekly, monthly, or annual reports

Income Tracking

WealthWise makes it easy to record and categorize your income sources. Set up recurring income entries for regular paychecks or add one-time deposits when they occur.

  • Multiple income categories
  • Recurring income tracking
  • Income history visualization
  • Tax category assignment

Expense Tracking

Track all your expenses with detailed categorization. Easily add expenses on the go and see where your money is going at a glance.

  • Multiple expense categories
  • Recurring expense setup
  • Receipt photo storage
  • Location-based expense tracking

Financial Analytics

Gain insights into your financial habits with comprehensive analytics. Visualize spending patterns and identify areas for improvement.

  • Spending breakdown by category
  • Monthly/yearly comparison
  • Income vs. expense analysis
  • Trend visualization

Budget Planning

Set financial goals and track your progress with our budgeting tools. Create category-specific budgets and get alerts when you're approaching limits.

  • Custom budget creation
  • Budget vs. actual comparison
  • Goal setting and tracking
  • Budget alerts and notifications
